Print

Business Cards

Printed on one or both sides at the standard 3.5 x 2 inch size. The finish decides how the card looks and feels in the hand.

Types of business cards

9 options, each explained. Not sure which you need? Tell us what it is for and we will suggest one.

Finishes

Matte
A smooth card with no shine. Text stays easy to read under any light, and you can write on it.
Glossy
A shiny coating that makes colours and photos look brighter and richer.
Soft Touch
A velvety coating with a smooth, suede-like feel in the hand.
Spot UV
A clear, glossy coating on selected areas, such as your logo, so they shine against a matte card.
Foil
Metallic gold or silver foil pressed onto your logo or name so it catches the light.
Embossed
Your logo or name raised out of the card so you can feel it under your thumb.

Options

Single or Double Sided
Print on the front only, or use the back for a map, a tagline or a second language.
Rounded Corners
Corners trimmed to a soft curve instead of a sharp point.
Thick Stock
A heavier card that feels more substantial in the hand.

Spiral Binding

Reports, manuals and menus bound into a finished document, with a cover that protects it.

Types of spiral binding

7 options, each explained. Not sure which you need? Tell us what it is for and we will suggest one.

Binding

Spiral (Coil) Binding
A plastic coil threaded through holes along the edge. The pages turn fully and lie flat.
Cerlox (Comb) Binding
A plastic comb with teeth that clip through the pages. Pages can be added or removed later.
Wire-O Binding
A metal double-wire binding with a neat, professional look.
Stapled Booklets
Pages folded and stapled along the spine, for programs, catalogues and short guides.

Covers and Finishing

Clear Front Cover
A clear plastic sheet on the front so the title page shows through.
Card Back Cover
A stiff card on the back so the document holds its shape.
Laminating
A clear protective layer for menus, signs and certificates.
