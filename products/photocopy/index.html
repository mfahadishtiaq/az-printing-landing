Types of photocopy

5 options, each explained. Not sure which you need? Tell us what it is for and we will suggest one.

Black and White Copies
Everyday copies of documents, forms and handouts.
Colour Copies
Full-colour copies for flyers, photos and presentations.
Double Sided
Both sides of the page, to halve the paper.
Letter, Legal and Tabloid
The three standard page sizes: 8.5 x 11, 8.5 x 14 and 11 x 17.

Send your file to us or bring it on a USB stick and we print it at the counter.
